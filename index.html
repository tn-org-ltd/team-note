Privacy Policy

App: Team Notes · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

Team Notes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The notes you write and the lists of names you enter are stored locally on your device so they are available next time you open the app. This data is never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete notes inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It works fully offline and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Network access and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet and contains no third-party services or SDKs that collect data.

Clipboard

If you tap "Copy" in the Team Splitter, the team list is placed on your device clipboard. This happens only on your action, and the app does not read your clipboard.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
